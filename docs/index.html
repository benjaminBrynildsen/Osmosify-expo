Each row's icon gets its own colored chip. The "Why Noah?" page tells the Webster story.

A note on this draft: Fraunces (variable serif) carries the Noah wordmark and the words being learned. Nunito (rounded sans) does the rest. Caveat sneaks in for warm, hand-felt moments. The palette stays Webster-blue + embossed-gold but adds soft coral, sage, lavender, and sky for kid energy. Each child gets their own color initial.
